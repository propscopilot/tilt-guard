PropsCopilot Tilt Guard for MetaTrader 5 - set up once, about 2 minutes

1. Copy PropsCopilotGuard.ex5 into MetaTrader: File > Open Data Folder > MQL5 > Experts.
   Back in MetaTrader, right-click "Expert Advisors" in the Navigator and choose Refresh.
2. Tools > Options > Expert Advisors: tick "Allow WebRequest for listed URL" and add
   https://propscopilot.com
3. Drag PropsCopilotGuard onto ONE chart of the account. On the Inputs tab paste your guard key
   (propscopilot.com/guard shows it any time). On the Common tab tick "Allow Algo Trading". OK.
4. Make sure the Algo Trading button on the toolbar is green.

That's all. The guard reads the account by itself: the firm from your broker's server, the account size from
your first deposit, and on a demo account it sets common challenge rules so you can try it straight away.
The account appears in your cockpit within seconds; press "Looks right", or tap another challenge.

Good to know
- The panel sits in the chart's bottom-left corner, clear of the one-click trading buttons.
- Exits, stops and flatten are never blocked. Locks apply to this account only.
- The guard works while this terminal is open with Algo Trading on. For always-on protection, run
  MetaTrader on a VPS. If the guard goes offline during a lock, we email you.
- Your locks are kept on our server: removing or reinstalling the guard does not lift a lock.
- No broker password is ever asked for or sent anywhere. Try it on a demo first, and check your
  prop firm allows a risk-manager EA (most do). The guard never opens trades.

Other platforms (same guard key, same cockpit; steps at propscopilot.com/guard)
- MetaTrader 4: PropsCopilotGuard.ex4 into File > Open Data Folder > MQL4 > Experts, Refresh the Navigator, allow
  WebRequest for https://propscopilot.com, drag onto one chart, paste your key, AutoTrading on.
- cTrader: double-click PropsCopilotGuard.algo, start it on one chart with your key in "Guard key".
- NinjaTrader 8: download PropsCopilotGuard.cs while signed in (your key is inside), save it in
  Documents\NinjaTrader 8\bin\Custom\AddOns, then NinjaScript Editor > F5. It guards accounts on live connections.
- Web platforms (TradeLocker, Match-Trader, DXtrade, Tradovate, TopstepX, TradingView's trading panel): the browser
  guard for Chrome, Edge or Brave. Unzip, chrome://extensions > Developer mode > Load unpacked, paste your key in
  the Tilt Guard icon. In the browser the guard stops a refused order before it is sent.
